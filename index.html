About IBAC

IBAC is an independent, non-governmental, faith-based accreditation commission established to evaluate and promote quality, integrity, effectiveness, and continuous improvement in biblical and theological education.

Our Mission

To advance excellence, integrity, accountability, and continuous improvement through independent accreditation, evaluation, and quality assurance.

Our Vision

A global community of credible, academically sound, spiritually responsible, and continuously improving biblical and theological institutions.

Our Values

Integrity, excellence, accountability, fairness, academic quality, independence, Christian commitment, and continuous improvement.

Accreditation Process

IBAC accreditation is evidence-based and follows a defined review process.

Eligibility

Institution submits preliminary information for an eligibility review.

Application & Self-Study

The institution submits an application, self-study, and supporting evidence.

Evaluation

A qualified evaluation team reviews institutional practices against published standards.

Commission Decision

The appropriate IBAC body considers the evidence and makes an accreditation decision.

Public Recognition

Accredited institutions are listed in the official IBAC accreditation register.

Continuous Review

Institutions undergo periodic review and demonstrate ongoing compliance and improvement.

IBAC Accreditation Standards

Institutions are evaluated against published standards designed to support educational quality and institutional integrity.

Governance

Mission, leadership, governance, policies, accountability, and legal compliance.

Academic Programs

Curriculum quality, learning outcomes, assessment, academic integrity, and program effectiveness.

Faculty

Qualified faculty, teaching effectiveness, professional development, and scholarly engagement.

Students

Admissions, student records, support services, academic progress, and student protections.

Resources

Library, learning resources, facilities, technology, and financial sustainability.

Quality Assurance

Assessment, institutional effectiveness, strategic planning, and continuous improvement.

Transparency statement: IBAC accreditation does not by itself constitute government licensing, degree recognition, or recognition by another accreditation organization. Institutions must comply with applicable laws and regulations.

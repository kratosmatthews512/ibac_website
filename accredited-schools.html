Accredited Schools

The official public register of institutions formally listed by the Independent Bible Accreditation Commission.

Official Accreditation Register

This register provides transparent public information regarding institutions whose accreditation status has been formally approved and published by IBAC.

Important Notice: Only institutions that have received a formal accreditation decision should be listed as accredited. Applicant, pending, candidate, expired, withdrawn, or unverified institutions should not be represented as currently accredited.
